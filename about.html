About Eden Tree

Discover our journey from a small farm to Ghana's leading fresh produce supplier

Our Story

From a Vision to Ghana's Fresh Standard

Founded in 1996 by Catherine Krobo-Edusei, Eden Tree began as a small farm with a bold vision: to bring fresh, high-quality fruits and vegetables directly from Ghanaian farms to the tables of families across the country.

What started as a single farm in the Eastern Region of Ghana has grown into the nation's leading fresh produce company. Our commitment to quality and freshness has remained unchanged for over two decades, as we continue to source the finest produce from local farmers.

Today, Eden Tree operates from a state-of-the-art facility in Tema, supplying major supermarkets, restaurants, and institutions across Ghana. We take pride in supporting local agriculture while delivering the freshest produce to our customers.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide the freshest, highest-quality fruits and vegetables to Ghanaian families and businesses while supporting sustainable farming practices and empowering local farmers.

Our Vision

To become West Africa's most trusted fresh produce brand, setting the standard for quality, freshness, and sustainability in agricultural supply chains across the region.
