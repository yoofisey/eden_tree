Who We Are

We Promote Healthy Eating Habits

Eden Tree Ltd. is Ghana's leading fresh produce company, established with a mission to make nutritious, farm-fresh fruits and vegetables accessible to every household. We work directly with over 50 local farmers who share our passion for sustainable agriculture.

From our state-of-the-art packing facility in Accra, we sort, wash, pack, and distribute a wide variety of produce — ensuring every item meets our rigorous quality and food safety standards before it reaches your kitchen.
